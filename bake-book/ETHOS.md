# Design ethos (fulcrumRust)

Parked from Evan (2026-09-06).

## Tough but fair
- Readable hotspots / scars you can learn
- No softlocks from growth or fog-of-war tricks
- Honest HoB / recoil (no fake bloom cheat)
- Heat you can read; extracts you earn
- Avoid Forever Winter–style dark patterns in the loop (respect the peers; don’t copy the unfair grind)

## Pastiche with respect
- Steal **language** (dither, grit, calcified overgrowth, floor spills) from Sulfur / Forever Winter / Beta Decay / FoW
- Don’t steal their maps or career layers
- Locus growth scars stay ours
